# Hi, I'm Avishek Kedia

I'm the founder of [Airful](https://airful.io), where we build digital products and operating systems that help teams turn complex workflows into useful, dependable software.

I work across product strategy, engineering and growth—from the first conversation about a problem through building, reviewing and improving the systems people use every day.

## What I work on

- **Operational platforms:** custom applications, CRMs and connected workflows built around how teams actually work.
- **AI-assisted systems:** practical ways to use AI and agents in research, development and operations, with human judgment in the loop.
- **Data and growth:** connecting information, measurement and execution so teams can make better decisions and keep improving.
